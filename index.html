Future Steps

🔬 Expand Research

Conduct larger-scale studies with a more diverse college population and partner with UCI's psychology department to clinically validate our tempo findings.

🧩 Adaptive Solutions

Build a feature that creates a personalized study playlist based on user’s questionnaire results, sequencing slow-to-fast or fast-to-slow tempo music based on what helped them most.

🎼 Platform Integration

Future iterations may include an interactive app, real-time music recommendations based on user mood or focus level, and broader research with a larger participant group to better understand how music tempo can support everyday well-being.

🏫 Campus Outreach

Partner with UCI's Student Wellness Center to introduce TEMPO as a mental health resource, and present our research at undergraduate research conferences.

What is...

An interactive listening experience that reveals how the speed of music changes the way you think, feel, and experience time.



Problem

College students use music daily to manage stress and focus, but they lack understanding of how it works. Without awareness of how tempo specifically affects their mental state, music remains an intuitive tool rather than an informed choice.
